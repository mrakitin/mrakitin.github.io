NYS Chemistry Regents Exam Prep

Mount Sinai High School — 11th Grade

A comprehensive, interactive study guide covering all 10 units of the New York State Chemistry Regents curriculum. Each unit includes a study guide and a practice quiz with instant feedback.

⚠️ Two exams are currently running in parallel (as of 2025–26)
New York State is transitioning between two versions of the Chemistry Regents. Check with your teacher which one applies to you:
  • Physical Setting/Chemistry (1996 Core Curriculum, traditional format) — nysedregents.org/Chemistry
  • Physical Science: Chemistry — NYSP12SLS (2016 NYS P-12 Science Learning Standards, NGSS-aligned) — nysedregents.org/ps-chemistry
The chemistry content (Units 1–10) is covered by both exams. The NYSP12SLS version emphasizes phenomenon-based questions and science & engineering practices.

Curriculum Units

Unit 01

Matter & Energy

Classification of matter, phase changes, heating curves, and heat calculations (q = mcΔT).

★☆☆⏱ 45–60 min
Unit 02

Atomic Structure

Subatomic particles, electron configuration, isotopes, atomic spectra, and the Bohr model.

★★☆⏱ 50–65 min
Unit 03

Periodic Table

Periodic trends (electronegativity, ionization energy, atomic radius), families, and element properties.

★☆☆⏱ 30–45 min
Unit 04

Chemical Bonding

Lewis structures, ionic and covalent bonds, VSEPR geometry, molecular polarity, and intermolecular forces.

★★★⏱ 60–75 min
Unit 05

Physical Behavior of Matter

States of matter, gas laws, solutions, molarity, colligative properties, and intermolecular forces.

★★★⏱ 60–75 min
Unit 06

Kinetics & Equilibrium

Reaction rates, activation energy, potential energy diagrams, and Le Chatelier's Principle.

★★☆⏱ 45–60 min
Unit 07

Organic Chemistry

Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, functional groups, naming, organic reactions, and polymers.

★★☆⏱ 45–60 min
Unit 08

Oxidation-Reduction

Oxidation numbers, half-reactions, balancing redox equations, and electrochemical cells.

★★★⏱ 50–65 min
Unit 09

Acids, Bases & Salts

Arrhenius and Brønsted-Lowry theories, pH scale, neutralization reactions, and titration.

★★☆⏱ 45–60 min
Unit 10

Nuclear Chemistry

Types of radioactive decay, nuclear equations, half-life calculations, and fission vs. fusion.

★☆☆⏱ 30–45 min

📅 Recommended 10-Week Study Schedule

  • Units 01 & 02: Matter and Energy + Atomic Structure
  • Units 03 & 04: Periodic Table + Chemical Bonding
  • Unit 05: Physical Behavior of Matter
  • Unit 06: Kinetics and Equilibrium
  • Unit 07: Organic Chemistry
  • Unit 08: Oxidation-Reduction
  • Unit 09: Acids, Bases, and Salts
  • Unit 10: Nuclear Chemistry
  • Review Reference Tables + weak areas

Exam Day Tips

✅ Use your Reference Tables actively Most calculation questions require them — Table T has all the formulas.
💡 Answer every question There is no penalty for wrong answers on the Regents exam.
⚠️ Show all work on constructed-response questions Partial credit is available even if your final answer is wrong.
