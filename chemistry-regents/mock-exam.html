Q1. Which of the following is a chemical change?
Burning produces new substances (CO₂, H₂O, ash) → chemical change.
Q2. How much heat is needed to raise 200. g of water from 20.0°C to 80.0°C? (c=4.18 J/g·°C)
q = (200.)(4.18)(60.0) = 50,160 J.
Q3. An atom of bromine has atomic number 35 and mass number 80. The number of neutrons is
Neutrons = mass # − atomic # = 80 − 35 = 45.
Q4. Which electron configuration is nitrogen (Z=7) in the ground state?
N has 7 electrons: 2 in first shell, 5 in second → 2-5.
Q5. Which element is in Period 3, Group 17?
Period 3 = 3rd row; Group 17 = halogens → Chlorine (Cl).
Q6. Compared to Cs, Li has
Li is above Cs in Group 1 → smaller → harder to remove valence electron → higher IE.
Q7. Which represents a nonpolar covalent bond?
H–H: ΔEN = 0 → nonpolar covalent.
Q8. What molecular shape has bond angles of ≈109.5°?
Tetrahedral (e.g., CH₄) has 4 equal bonding pairs → 109.5°.
Q9. A gas at 300 K and 2.0 atm occupies 4.0 L. Volume at 600 K and 2.0 atm?
Charles's Law: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ → 4.0/300 = V₂/600 → V₂ = 8.0 L.
Q10. Which solution has the lowest freezing point?
NaCl → 2 ions/formula unit → most particles → greatest freezing point depression.
Q11. Adding a catalyst to an equilibrium reaction will
Catalyst lowers Ea for both directions equally → no shift in position; equilibrium reached faster.
Q12. For: 2SO₂(g)+O₂(g)⇌2SO₃(g)+heat, increasing temperature shifts equilibrium
Exothermic reaction: heat is a product. Adding heat → shifts left toward reactants.
Q13. Which is an alkyne?
Alkynes: CₙH₂ₙ₋₂. For n=4: C₄H₆.
Q14. In 2Mg+O₂→2MgO, which is oxidized?
Mg: 0 → +2 (increase in oxidation #) → oxidized.
Q15. In a galvanic cell, which statement about the anode is correct?
Anode = Oxidation in ALL electrochemical cells (both galvanic and electrolytic).
Q16. A solution has pH=11. Which is correct?
pOH = 14−11 = 3 → [OH⁻] = 10⁻³ M. Solution is basic (pH > 7).
Q17. Which is a Brønsted-Lowry base?
NH₃ accepts H⁺ (proton) → Brønsted-Lowry base.
Q18. Which nuclear radiation has no mass and no charge?
Gamma: ⁰₀γ — mass number = 0, charge = 0.
Q19. Starting with 200. g of a radioisotope (half-life 5.0 days), how much remains after 20. days?
20/5 = 4 half-lives. 200 × (½)⁴ = 200/16 = 12.5 g.
Q20. When C-14 undergoes beta decay, the product is
β⁻ decay: atomic # +1 (6→7=N); mass # unchanged (14). Product: ¹⁴₇N.
Q21. % error = |2.65−2.70|/2.70 × 100 = ?
|2.65−2.70|/2.70 × 100 = 0.05/2.70 × 100 ≈ 1.9%.
Q22. An atom has 26 protons, 30 neutrons, and 24 electrons. Its charge is
26 protons − 24 electrons = +2 charge.
Q23. Atomic mass of X: X-10 (20%) and X-11 (80%) = ?
(0.20×10)+(0.80×11) = 2.0+8.8 = 10.8 amu.
Q24. A gas at 27°C (300 K), 100 kPa, 3.0 L. Volume at 227°C (500 K) and 200 kPa?
P₁V₁/T₁=P₂V₂/T₂ → (100)(3.0)/300=(200)V₂/500 → V₂ = (100×3.0×500)/(300×200) = 150000/60000 = 2.5 L.
Q25. M = 0.50 mol KCl / 250 mL solution = ?
M = 0.50 mol / 0.250 L = 2.0 M.
Q26. Correct Keq for N₂(g)+3H₂(g)⇌2NH₃(g)?
Keq = [products]^coeff/[reactants]^coeff = [NH₃]²/([N₂][H₂]³).
Q27. 25.0 mL NaOH + 37.5 mL of 0.200 M HCl to reach equivalence. Molarity of NaOH?
MₐVₐ=MᵦVᵦ → Mₐ(25.0)=(0.200)(37.5) → Mₐ=7.50/25.0=0.300 M.
Q28. Which nuclear equation represents alpha decay?
Alpha decay: mass −4, atomic # −2: ²²⁶₈₈Ra → ²²²₈₆Rn + ⁴₂He.
Q29. I-131 (half-life 8.07 days). Starting with 40.0 g, after 24.21 days?
n = 24.21/8.07 = 3. 40.0 × (½)³ = 40.0/8 = 5.00 g.
Q30. A Keq = 2.5×10⁻⁴ indicates
Keq << 1 → denominator (reactants) >> numerator (products) → reactants favored.
