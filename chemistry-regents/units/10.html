Q1. Which type of radiation has the greatest penetrating power?
Gamma radiation is pure energy (electromagnetic) — requires lead or thick concrete to stop.
Q2. When Ra-226 (Z=88) undergoes alpha decay, the product nucleus is
Alpha decay: mass − 4 → 222, atomic # − 2 → 86 = Radon (Rn). ²²⁶₈₈Ra → ²²²₈₆Rn + ⁴₂He.
Q3. In beta (β⁻) decay, the atomic number of the daughter nucleus
β⁻ decay: a neutron converts to a proton + electron. The nucleus gains 1 proton → atomic # increases by 1.
Q4. Starting with 80.0 g of a radioisotope with half-life 10 years, how much remains after 30 years?
30 yr / 10 yr = 3 half-lives. 80.0 × (1/2)³ = 80.0 × 1/8 = 10.0 g.
Q5. Nuclear fission is the process that occurs in
Fission = splitting heavy nuclei (U-235) → used in nuclear power plants. The sun uses fusion.
Q6. Carbon-14 is used for radioactive dating because
C-14 has a half-life of ~5,715 years and is incorporated into all living things through carbon cycle.
Q7. What is the missing particle in: ²³⁵₉₂U + ¹₀n → ¹⁴⁴₅₆Ba + X + 3¹₀n?
Mass: 236 = 144 + X + 3 → X mass = 89. Atomic #: 92 = 56 + X → X = 36. ⁸⁹₃₆Kr.
Q8. Iodine-131 has half-life 8.07 days. Starting with 40.0 g, how much remains after 24.21 days?
n = 24.21/8.07 = 3 half-lives. 40.0 × (1/2)³ = 40.0/8 = 5.00 g.
Q9. Compared to nuclear fission, nuclear fusion
Fusion combines light nuclei (H isotopes) → releases more energy per unit mass, with minimal radioactive waste.
Q10. Which nuclear emission has no mass and no charge?
Gamma ray symbol ⁰₀γ — mass number = 0, charge = 0.
