Organic Reactions

ReactionType of compoundDescription

EsterificationOrganic acid + alcohol→ Ester + water

$$\ce{C6H12O6 -> 2 C2H5OH + 2 CO2} \quad \text{(Fermentation)}$$
$$\ce{Organic\ acid + Alcohol -> Ester + H2O} \quad \text{(Esterification)}$$
⚠️ Common traps: Addition = unsaturated; Substitution = saturated. Esterification produces water. Carbon always forms exactly 4 bonds. Isomers have the SAME molecular formula but different structures.

Practice Quiz

0 / 0
Q1. Which is an unsaturated hydrocarbon?
C₂H₄ is ethene — an alkene with a C=C double bond → unsaturated.
Q2. The reaction of ethene (C₂H₄) with Br₂ to form C₂H₄Br₂ is an example of
An unsaturated compound reacting with Br₂ — the Br₂ adds across the double bond → addition reaction.
Q3. What is the correct name for CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₃?
4 carbons = but- + single bonds only = -ane → butane.
Q4. Fermentation of glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) produces
C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂ — fermentation produces ethanol (ethyl alcohol) and CO₂.
Q5. Which functional group identifies an organic acid?
The –COOH (carboxyl) group is the defining functional group of organic acids.
Q6. An organic acid reacts with an alcohol to form water and
Esterification: organic acid + alcohol → ester + water.
Q7. Two compounds have the molecular formula C₄H₁₀ but different structures. They are called
Same molecular formula, different structural arrangement → isomers.
Q8. Which is the general formula for alkynes?
Alkynes have a triple bond and general formula CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ (Table O in Reference Tables).
Q9. Polyethylene is made from many ethylene monomers. This is an example of
Ethylene (CH₂=CH₂) has a double bond — monomers add across it → addition polymerization.
Q10. What are the products of complete combustion of methane (CH₄)?
Complete combustion: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O.
